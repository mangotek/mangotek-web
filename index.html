08 — Questions

Before you ask.

What does a Wi-Fi audit include?

A predictive model, an on-site survey, and heatmaps for signal, SNR and roaming, with access-point placement recommendations.

Can you work in an occupied building?

Yes. We stage work by floor and after hours, and keep live systems running through cutovers.

Which platforms do you deploy?

UniFi networking, 3CX telephony, and professional audio-video and security platforms chosen to suit the brief.

Do you support what you install?

Yes. Monitoring, moves-adds-changes and response SLAs are part of every engagement.
